AI chip roadmap targets 80x compute

Tesla disclosed a revamped AI accelerator roadmap, renaming its chips from AI4/AI5/AI6 to SI4/SI5/SI6 ("Super Intelligence"). The sixth-gen inference chip SI6 is targeted at 80x the compute and 9x the memory of the current SI4 (HW4), an estimated 17,280–46,080 TOPS, due 2029–2030 on a Samsung 2nm-class process under a foundry agreement running through 2033.

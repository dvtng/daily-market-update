Hackers move $860M stolen Ether

Elliptic's report updated Friday Nov 20 found hackers linked to North Korea's TraderTraitor group moved about 488,000 ETH (about $860 million) stolen in October's $6.3 billion Binance theft, emptying five of the nine largest ether wallets tied to the attack — 74,880 ETH on Nov 17, 78,415 on Nov 18, 137,602 on Nov 19 and 197,460 on Nov 20 — redistributing the funds into smaller wallets of about 12,000 ETH each. About 39,000 ETH (about $70 million) was swapped into roughly 1,100 BTC via THORChain, and at least 2,310 BTC (about $149 million) from the theft has gone into coinjoins, about 90% of it since Nov 17. About $2.35 billion remains in wallets tied to the theft while $2.85 billion has been frozen or seized.

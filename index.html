Micron products exempt from new tariffs

The USTR's Section 301 tariff proposal, announced Nov 25, exempts servers, smartphones, PCs, storage, chips and most semiconductor manufacturing equipment from new duties on imported electronics; Micron's core lines are shielded, while headphones, lithium-ion batteries, printers, TVs and game consoles are not. The proposal is not final: public comments due Jan 5, 2027, hearings Jan 25, final action due March 11.

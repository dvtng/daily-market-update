Korean group plans FSD complaint

South Korean consumer group Citizens United for Consumer Sovereignty plans to file a complaint with the Korea Fair Trade Commission on Thursday, Nov 5, alleging Tesla misled tens of thousands of customers by marketing Full Self-Driving software that cannot legally be used in most of its vehicles in the country — FSD works on only about 20% of registered Teslas (US-built ones), while most are China-made and Tesla only recently began certification for those.

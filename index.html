WSJ: $12B in Nvidia chip orders for China

The Wall Street Journal reported that Singapore-based Megaspeed arranged nearly $12 billion in orders for top-end Nvidia chips intended for Chinese companies between March 2024 and June 2025, based on records reviewed by the Journal; the records cover several hundred thousand chips but do not show how much was actually delivered. Megaspeed says it complies with export controls; Nvidia says it works to prevent chip smuggling.

First-ever Starship Ship catch at tower

After orbiting Earth 14 times over ~22 hours and deploying 26 Starlink V3 satellites on Flight 15, Starship's upper stage (Ship 42) returned to Starbase, Texas, on Saturday afternoon and was caught in midair by the Mechazilla launch tower's chopstick arms — the first time a Starship upper stage has been recovered this way; the Super Heavy booster catch had already failed on Nov 13, so the double catch was not achieved, but the Ship catch is a key milestone toward full reusability, and NASA welcomed the result.

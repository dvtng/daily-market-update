Mercado Bitcoin reopens view-only after hack

Vitalik: value ETH as store of value

Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin said in an X thread on Sunday morning Nov 8 that ETH should be valued primarily as a store of value, arguing the case for valuing it as a fee-burning token was weaker than it appeared last cycle. He said EIP-1559's burn is "fine and good" and should stay, but ETH's value comes from decentralization, security and censorship resistance, not from fee burn offsetting issuance; daily burn has fallen sharply (about 1.48M ETH burned in 2022 vs ~19,200 ETH, ~$43M, so far this year).

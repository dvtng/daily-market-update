Glamsterdam upgrade faces contract-break pushback

Two developers filed rival proposals on Sunday Nov 8 to keep the Glamsterdam upgrade from breaking existing contracts: Leo Glisic's EIP-8488 (split instruction set, old contracts keep old gas schedule) and Giulio Rebuffo's draft (dual gas meters). The concern is EIP-8037/EIP-8038 gas repricing (contract-creation base charge rising from 32,000 to 183,600 gas); security researchers say ~200,000 existing contracts could be affected, including immutable ones holding user funds. Curve founder Michael Egorov warned on X (Nov 7) of a possible emergency hard fork; investor Ryan Berckmans reversed his support for a fast launch (Nov 7). Developers had already postponed the Oct 26 Hoodi testnet activation; a private test was tentatively discussed for Nov 16–20.
